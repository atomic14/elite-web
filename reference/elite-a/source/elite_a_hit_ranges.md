# Elite-A hits to destroy by player ship and laser

Each named NPC/object can have different energy, defence, laser and missile values in different S.A-S.W blueprint files. These tables therefore show the **minimum-maximum number of registered hits across all released variants**, with regeneration excluded. Exact per-file rows are in `elite_a_hits_to_destroy.json`.

`immune` means a station. `no damage` means that laser cannot overcome the target defence. A trailing `*` means at least one variant takes no damage while another variant can be destroyed.
